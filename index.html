About Us

Solar energy built for Filipino homes and businesses.

SINAGLIKHA is a solar energy company that works across the solar value chain, from producing photovoltaic cells to assembling modules and bringing affordable systems to the people who need them.

Our name and our identity draw on Filipino creativity and warmth. We believe reliable, sustainable energy should feel within reach for families, entrepreneurs, and communities, and we organize our work, and our people, around that belief.

  • Practical. Solutions designed around real household and business needs.
  • Reliable. Quality assurance and support that stand behind every system.
  • Sustainable. Renewable energy and responsible consumption, together.

PV Cell Manufacturing

Production and manufacturing of photovoltaic (PV) solar cells.

Module Assembly

Assembly of solar cells into high-efficiency solar modules.

Sales & Distribution

Sale and distribution of affordable solar panels and sustainable energy solutions.

Quality & Support

Quality assurance, maintenance, and support services for solar energy systems.

Our Mission

Company Mission

Our Vision

Company Vision
