Our Mission

Solar that’s practical and within reach.

Read the full statement

SINAGLIKHA is committed to making solar energy practical and accessible to Filipino households and businesses by providing reliable, cost-effective, and sustainable energy solutions. We also promote renewable-energy awareness and responsible consumption by equipping customers with clear information that enables them to make informed energy decisions.

Our Vision

A brighter future for every community.

Read the full statement

To contribute to a future in which reliable and sustainable solar-energy solutions are accessible to more Filipino households, businesses, and communities, empowering lives and creating opportunities for inclusive growth.

Human Resources

HR Mission

To attract, develop, engage, and retain a skilled, ethical, safety-conscious, and future-ready workforce through fair opportunities, continuous learning, meaningful collaboration, and employee support, enabling SINAGLIKHA to deliver reliable solar-energy solutions and sustainable value to its customers and communities.

To cultivate a highly skilled, safety-driven, innovative, and values-centered workforce that positions SINAGLIKHA as a preferred employer and trusted contributor to the Philippine renewable-energy sector.
