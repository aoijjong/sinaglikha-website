About Us

Sinag+ Likha

Company Profile

SINAGLIKHA is a Filipino renewable-energy company that designs, manufactures, supplies, installs, and maintains reliable solar-energy systems for residential, commercial, and industrial clients. Through quality products, responsible sourcing, technical assessment, after-sales support, and customer education, the company seeks to make solar energy more practical, affordable, and accessible while promoting responsible energy consumption and environmental sustainability.

Sinag — a ray of light. Sunlight, hope, energy, and new opportunities.

Likha — to create. Creativity, innovation, and Filipino ingenuity.

Our Mission

Solar That’s Practical and Within Reach

Read the Full Statement

SINAGLIKHA is committed to making solar energy practical and accessible to Filipino households and businesses by providing reliable, cost-effective, and sustainable energy solutions. We also promote renewable-energy awareness and responsible consumption by equipping customers with clear information that enables them to make informed energy decisions.

Our Vision

A Brighter Future for Every Community

Read the Full Statement

To contribute to a future in which reliable and sustainable solar-energy solutions are accessible to more Filipino households, businesses, and communities, empowering lives and creating opportunities for inclusive growth.

Objectives

Reliable Solar, Within Reach

To improve access to reliable, cost-effective, and sustainable solar energy solutions for Filipino households and businesses through quality manufacturing, installation, and maintenance, while promoting responsible energy consumption and customer satisfaction during our initial years of operations.

01

Quality and Cost-Effective Solar Solutions

Reliable, affordable systems that fit the energy needs and budgets of residential, commercial, and industrial customers.

02

Product Quality and Durability

Standardized manufacturing, quality inspections, reliable installation, regular maintenance, and dependable after-sales support.

03

Market Accessibility and Expansion

Better sales, distribution, delivery, and installation, with steady growth in access to solar energy.

04

Responsible Material Sourcing

Reliable supplier partnerships, with the quality, cost, and sustainability of raw materials evaluated.

05

Renewable Energy Awareness

Customer education on the benefits of solar, proper maintenance, and responsible energy use, through consultations and learning resources.

06

Sustainable Business Growth

Stronger customer satisfaction, monitored business performance, competitive pricing, and opportunities for gradual expansion.

Core Values

What We Stand For

Seven values behind everything we make and do.

  1. Integrity in Every Action

    Honesty, transparency, and accountability guide every decision, building trust with our employees, customers, partners, and communities.

  2. Safety in Every Operation

    We protect our Likhains, partners, and communities with proper safety standards in manufacturing, handling, transportation, installation, and maintenance.

  3. Excellence in Everything

    High standards, quality workmanship, and dependable service, with continuous improvement that creates value for our customers and company.

  4. Innovation for a Brighter Future

    Creativity, employee initiative, and technological development drive continuous improvement in our solar products and operations.

  5. Inclusivity in Every Connection

    Respect, equal opportunity, and collaboration make solar energy accessible to people from every background and circumstance.

  6. Commitment to Our Likhains

    Likhains are our employees. We empower them, and their contributions help us deliver responsive service and better customer experiences.

  7. Responsibility for Our Environment

    Care for the environment across the product lifecycle: sourcing, manufacturing, packaging, transportation, installation, maintenance, and end-of-life management.
